# SOP: Customer service

Covers email and Shopify Inbox replies on PostureWell. Bar: every customer message gets a first response inside one business day, and every issue has a clear resolution path on first reply.

## Channels

- Support inbox: `support@posturewell.example` (Gmail)
- Shopify Inbox (chat widget)
- Instagram and TikTok DMs (lower priority, replied to within 48h)

## Tone

Warm, plain English, no jargon. Lead with what we are doing for the customer, then the explanation. Never blame the supplier or shipping carrier by name to the customer.

## Common scenarios

### Where is my order

1. Look up the order in Shopify and confirm the tracking number is populated.
2. Open the carrier page in a private tab and read the latest scan.
3. Reply with: the latest scan date and city, the expected delivery window from `suppliers/dsers-mapping.md`, and a promise to follow up if no new scan appears in 5 days.

### Item arrived damaged or wrong

1. Ask for a photo if not already attached.
2. Issue a free replacement via DSers without asking for a return (return shipping costs more than the unit).
3. Log the SKU and supplier in the defect-tracking note so we can roll up monthly defect rate per supplier.

### Refund request inside 30 days

1. Confirm the order is within the 30-day window from delivery (or from order date if not yet delivered).
2. Refund in full via Shopify Admin.
3. Do not require the buyer to return cheap items (<$25 unit cost) - it costs more than it saves.

### Refund request after 30 days

1. Offer a 20 percent store credit instead of a cash refund.
2. If the buyer pushes back, escalate to the owner before agreeing to a cash refund.

### Chargeback notification

1. Stop, do not reply to the buyer.
2. Gather: order details, tracking proof, delivery confirmation, all prior buyer comms.
3. Submit evidence in Shopify Payments within 24h.
